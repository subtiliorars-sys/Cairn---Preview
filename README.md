# Cairn

*An adventure that leads somewhere real.*

A cairn is a stack of trail stones — quiet markers left by whoever came before, showing the
way for whoever comes next.

Cairn is a game, built with care: a calm world of quests, community, and discovery that
quietly reconnects you to real life and real people. We're building something gentle.

More soon.

## What to expect
- **A quiet place.** No accounts to look around. No tracking, no analytics, no third-party
  scripts — the absence is the point.
- **Nothing permanent.** You can look without being seen. You're never put on a list.
- **Take what you need.** Come and go on your own terms.
