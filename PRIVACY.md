# Privacy

We keep this simple.

- **No accounts** to land here, and **no tracking** — no analytics, no third-party scripts.
  For us the absence is the trust signal, not a banner about it.
- **Nothing is permanent.** You can look without being seen, and you're never on a list.
- **What's yours stays yours.** If you ever choose to share something with us, you can take
  it back or have it erased.

A full privacy policy will follow before anything opens up.
